Larva Seconds Lost

Larva stats for your Zerg games (larva generated and missed, inject uptime) per game phase: for one replay, or averaged over many replays.

What the numbers mean

  • Phases start when your Workers Active count goes above 36 (Mid), 66 (Late) and 89 (End). It is the same count as Scelight's Workers Active chart (Charts, Player stats): drones at work, not in eggs or buildings. Phases never go back.
  • Missed injects (dark red lane): a hatchery is red while it is not injected and one of your queens is within 12 cells of it with 25 energy she does not use within the next 29 seconds. Where she is comes from the replay: she was born there, injected it, placed a creep tumor next to it, was seen there while taking damage, or you ordered her there. A queen stays where she is until you order her away, so a queen left idle at a hatchery stays red, and a queen you send across the map is not. (For a player whose queen commands the replay does not show, such as the computer, she counts as there for 29 seconds after each sign, at most 90 seconds between two signs at the same hatchery.) A queen between two hatcheries makes only the nearer one red at a time. Energy she spends on creep tumors or transfusions within 29 seconds is not missed. Every 29 seconds of red is one missed inject (3 larva). Hover a red window to see which queen and which evidence made it.
  • Times and rates use the game clock, as in the StarCraft II replay viewer. lrv / htch / min means larva per hatchery per minute of game time (60 seconds on that clock). Scelight's own pages show times about 1 % higher (7:56 for 7:51).
  • Rankings rate larva generated and larva missed (Early, Mid, Late) against ladder games by league (the player's MMR and the league limits of MMR-Ranges, silver to GM) and pro games.
  • Milestones (on a replay page, right under the player's name, above the larva summary and the phase table): when you first had 44, 55, 66 and 80 drones (Workers Active) and 100, 150 and 200 supply used. The ranking row compares each time with ladder and pro games of the same matchup that reached it: top 30% masters is a time inside the masters tier (the league is bold whenever you are inside a tier). A tier can name two leagues, plat/dia: plat gets there as early as dia, so both share it. Where only one league has enough games, the ranking is that league's pace: GM/pro pace 6:47 means you were there by then. A time later than every tier reads aim for 9:01: the time where the slowest tier with enough games starts, your next goal. Games that never got there are not in the bands, so this rates how fast you got there; how often you get there counts as much. n/a: not reached, or too few games in that matchup (80 drones in ZvZ, for example).

Hub and sub-pages

The Larva page is a hub with buttons only; showing it never analyzes anything. Every result opens as a sub-page under Larva in the navigation tree. Close a sub-page with the X in its title bar or with Ctrl+F4; this also stops its background work. The hub cannot be closed.

  • Open Replay... opens a replay file on a new page named after the file. A replay that is already open is only selected, not opened twice. Refresh Current Replay re-analyzes the page's own replay.
  • Analyze Latest Replay opens the most recently detected replay.
  • Multi Analysis... opens a multi analysis page (below). Ladder games only is checked on every start and is not remembered.
  • Both file choosers remember the folder of your last confirmed selection, also after a restart. If it is gone they start in the folder of the last detected replay.

New replays open automatically

When Scelight detects a new replay with at least one Zerg player (any game type and length), it is analyzed in the background and added as a sub-page. It is selected only if you are on the Larva hub or a Larva sub-page; otherwise it just appears in the tree. Other rules: no second page for an open replay; no automatic page while 20 replay pages are open; at most 5 new replays wait at a time, the extra ones are skipped (see the log) and can be opened by hand; no page if the replay cannot be read or has no Zerg player. There is no on/off switch.

Multi analysis

Pick any number of replays. The page shows progress and, when done, is named from <first date> to <last date> (dates from the replays). Above 500 files you must confirm. Closing the page cancels the run.

Eligible games: parsed OK, 1v1, with Ladder games only checked a ladder game (unchecked: any mode), at least 4:00 long (5376 game loops), and at least one Zerg player. The line under the header counts what each rule excluded. Replays that could not be parsed, and games without a phase table, are listed with the reason under Skipped replays.

Tracked players: accounts, not names, so a renamed player counts once. The Zerg account with the most games is always tracked; a second one only with at least 3 games and 20% of all eligible games. At most 2, shown as Name (REGION), the one with more games on the left.

Tables per player: Milestones first, then All games, ZvP, ZvT, ZvZ.

  • Avg phase end [m:ss]: average game time at which the phase ended, over the games that reached it, shown as m:ss (k/n): k of n games reached it. Games that never reached it are left out, not counted as the game end.
  • Inject uptime and the larva rows are pooled: counts and hatchery time of all games are added up, as if it were one long game, so longer games weigh more. They are not averages of per-game values.
  • Milestones: 44, 55, 66 and 80 drones, 100, 150 and 200 supply, each the median m:ss (k/n) over the games that got there (one slow game does not move it), with a ranking row ("  ranking) per matchup. k/n shows how often you got there; the ranking ignores the games that did not. All mixes your matchups and uses the bands of all games, so a player with many ZvZ rates lower there (ZvZ reaches drone counts later).
  • No data shows n/a.

Caveats. The ranking rows rate an average (milestones: a median) with bands made for single games, so it sits closer to the middle than single games do; it is not a percentile. Supply and workers are recorded only every 160 game loops (7.1 s on the game clock), so all milestone times can be that much late, and two leagues one sample apart are only 7 s apart.

Updates

The bottom row of the Larva page shows your version. Once a day (and when you click Check now) the module reads https://minetonight.github.io/scelight/larva-seconds-lost/module.xml; no data about you or your replays is sent. When a newer version is out, click Install: it is downloaded, checked against its published checksums and put next to your current version. Restart Scelight to use it; the old version folder is removed on that start. Hide hides that version until a newer one is out. Untick Check for updates automatically to stop the daily check. If an update fails, nothing is changed; use Open download page to update by hand.

Development: start Scelight with -Dlarva.dev.dump.enabled=true for a diagnostic dump (path override -Dlarva.dev.dump.file=...; default: LarvaSecondsLost-dev-dump.txt in the home folder). If startup fails, check the log for the initialization phase.
